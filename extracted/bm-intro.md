# Bubble Mastery — Course Introduction

## Overview
Introduction to the Bubble Mastery course by Ryan (NungAi89). Covers the approach to studying ICM and bubble play, why the bubble is the most important area to study, and how to use the Pareto principle to structure study efficiently.

## Key Concepts
- The bubble is where MTT ROI lives — you see it far more often than the final table
- Study the perimeters (extremes) first: ChipEV (first hand) and direct bubble. Spend ~40% on each, ~20% in the middle. Everything stems from the extremes
- If you master the bubble (highest ICM spot), you can extrapolate to near-bubble and post-bubble
- Break study into chunks: master one area, then move to the next. Don't study everything at once
- Studying different formats (sit-and-gos, satellites, MTTs) reveals blind spots in other formats
- Distill complex game trees into a small number of representative spots (e.g. 30 3-bet pot configurations → 5 spots)
- Use logic and intuition to fill in blanks between perimeters, then check hands to verify

## Source Quotes
- "the bubble in itself is a chunk of the game that is very important because a lot of our ROI is going to come from not screwing this up"
- "study 40% on one perimeter, study 40% on the other, and then study 20 in the middle"
- "if you just study ICM from like the highest ICM spot in the game, then it's easy to back up and extrapolate"
