# Foreword

## Overview
Intro to the Simple Poker Systems bundle. The instructor explains the approach: systems developed from years of coaching, targeting the most common and impactful mistakes in students' games.

## Key Concepts
- The systems coalesce around specific areas where mistakes cluster — these are the most common leaks the instructor has identified across students
- These are also the most impactful mistakes — the ones that materially affect win rates
- The backbone of private coaching is everything in this bundle
- Systems are designed to be basic, repeatable ways to think about spots and group decisions together
