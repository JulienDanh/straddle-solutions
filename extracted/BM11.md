# BM11 — Polar Opens · Split Range C-Betting

## Overview
BTN polar open strategy on short stacks with split range c-betting postflop. The ICM model really doesn't like putting in money with marginal holdings. Risk factors present in chips get amplified. Jamming flops becomes more significant. Checking back with some strong hands to protect turn/river range.

## Buckets
- **Polar opens (preflop)**: BTN opens a split range — some min-raises, some open shoves. The split depends on BB stack depth.
- **Large bet region (postflop)**: Boards like AQ2 two-tone get a very large bet (~105%+ pot). Protection from flush draws. NOT gut shots — it's the flush draws that drive the overbet.
- **Check-back region (postflop)**: Monotone flops, boards with draws. More checking back vs ChipEV. Don't bloat the pot with thin value.

## Decision Rules
- **Risk factors amplified**: Paired boards (not 886), connectedness, flush draw presence, monotone flops — all lead to more checking. Any risk factor in chips gets amplified in ICM.
- **Monotone = lots of checking back**: Don't bet small and bloat the pot facing lower SPR on turn where you must defend weak parts of range.
- **Avoid stacking off with marginal hands**: ICM doesn't like putting in money with meh value. In chips, bet-calling second pair is fine. In ICM, it's rough.
- **Check back some strong hands**: If you only check back weak hands on T-high/J-high boards, your range is vulnerable on the turn. Pull some top pair into the check-back range to protect.
- **Jamming flops more significant**: Especially on boards front-loaded in the 23bb BTN-covered-by-BB training. Boards like AQ2 rainbow get large bets.
- **Flush draws drive overbet, not gut shots**: In the 23bb BTN covering BB spot, AQ2 rainbow gets a large bet. In this (covered) spot, AQ2 two-tone gets the large bet. The flush draw is the driver, not the gut shot.
- **Board texture sensitivity**: KQ6 rainbow vs KQ6 two-tone = vastly different strategies. In chips, both probably bet. In ICM, two-tone checks much more.

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Paired boards (not 886) | More checking |
| Connectedness / draws | More checking |
| Flush draw presence | Increases checking and jam sizing frequency |
| Monotone flops | Big risk — lots of checking back vs ChipEV |
| Two-tone vs rainbow | Vastly different strategies — two-tone checks much more |
| ICM pressure | Amplifies ALL risk factors |

## Sizing
- Large bet region: ~105%+ pot (protection from flush draws, not gut shots)
- Check-back region: check (don't bloat pot with thin value)
- Jamming flops: more significant than ChipEV on specific textures

## Common Leaks
- Bloating the pot with thin/medium value hands (fine in chips, rough in ICM)
- Not checking back enough strong hands to protect turn/river range
- Treating two-tone and rainbow boards the same (vastly different in ICM)
- Betting small on monotone flops (creates bad SPR on turn with weak range)
