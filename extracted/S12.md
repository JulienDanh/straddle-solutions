# System 12 — Defending 3-Bets OOP

## Overview
Hero opens from EP/MP, faces a 3-bet, calls, and is OOP on the flop. Core skill: two buckets — high boards (fold a lot) and low boards (defend robustly). Simple: be nitty on T-high+, be gangster on 9-high and below.

## Buckets
- **T-high+ (Broadway boards)**: Fold heavily — villain's 3-bet range dominates. Special emphasis on ace and king high.
- **9-high & below**: Defend robustly — villain misses these boards, your range is relatively stronger.

## Decision Rules
- **High boards (T+)**: Fold much more than MDF suggests. Basic MDF says 25% fold vs 1/3 bet; actual fold is 50%+ on ace/king high. Over-defending pocket pairs and weak draws is the most common mistake.
- **Low boards (9-)**: Defend wide. Play pairs, gut shots, backdoor draws. Be "gangster" — sticky and resilient.
- **Ace and king high = danger zone**: These are the worst boards. Fold even strong-looking hands (AJs with BDFD = worth zero on AJ4 vs a 3-bet).
- **Pocket pairs on K-high**: 77, 66, 55 = heavy folds. Even TT mixes to fold. Not worth defending vs a strong 3-bet range.
- **When facing a jam on low boards**: Call with pairs. If neither player hits the board (e.g. 963), your pair is good. Pot odds + range analysis = call.

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Ace-high flop | Maximum danger — fold heavily |
| King-high flop | Maximum danger — fold heavily |
| T-high+ generally | Fold more than MDF |
| Paired board (low) | Slightly elevated fold — can't use paired card |
| Disconnected low board | Elevated fold — no straight draws available |
| Deuce on board | Deuce favors the 3-bettor (neither hits it, but 3-bettor was ahead preflop) |
| Connected low board | Defend more — straight draws supplement |
| Small c-bet (20-25%) | Still fold heavily on high boards — MDF doesn't apply normally here |

## Sizing
- Villain c-bets 20-33% pot (small sizing, standard for 3-bet pots)
- MDF says fold 20-25%, but actual fold on ace/king high is 50%+
- On low boards, actual fold ≈ MDF (25-27%)

## Common Leaks
- Over-defending pocket pairs on ace/king high — 77, 88, 99 look playable but are heavy folds
- Over-defending ace-high with BDFD — AJs with BDFD is worth zero on AJ4 vs a 3-bet
- Under-defending low boards — folding pairs and high cards that are actually profitable
- Applying MDF blindly — the 3-bettor's range is too strong on high boards for basic MDF to work

## Hand Examples
- **AJs on 842 (EP vs HJ 3-bet, 20% c-bet)**: Fold. Two low cards help, but AJs is still worth zero vs a tight 3-bet range. Solver: pure fold.
- **77 on K84r (EP vs BTN 3-bet, 20% c-bet)**: Fold. King-high = danger zone. 77, 66, 55 = heavy folds. Even TT mixes to fold.
- **QTs on A85 (EP vs HJ 3-bet)**: Fold. Ace-high = maximum danger. Pocket nines = pure fold. KQ♥ = fold.
- **55 on 984 (EP 3-bet caller, 33% c-bet)**: Call. Low board = favorable. 55 is worth 100+ bb/100. Defend gut shots, KQs with BDFD, ATs with 3-to-straight.
- **44 on 963 (EP vs BTN 3-bet, jam)**: Call. Neither player hits this board. 44 is pure call. Pair vs pair, we're ahead often enough.
- **AQo on 752 (EP vs BTN 3-bet, 25bb, 25% c-bet)**: Call or shove. AQ is worth 186 bb/100. Low board = hero is resilient.

## Heuristics
- "T-high+: be nitty. 9-: be gangster."
- "Ace and king high = danger zone — fold heavily"
- "MDF doesn't apply normally on high boards vs 3-bets"
- "Low boards = defend wide, be sticky"
- "If neither player hits the board, the preflop favorite wins"
