# BM2 — Opening Into Covered Stacks

## Overview
A stack (or several) behind covers you. You open much tighter than baseline, with the degree of tightness driven by how much the BB covers you, position of covering stacks, and presence of shorter stacks elsewhere.

## Buckets
- **Covered by heaps (BB 2x+ you)**: Open very tight — they can destroy you postflop, donk, float
- **Covered but close (game of chicken)**: Open a bit wider — BB risks tournament life playing back
- **You cover the rest, covered by one**: Still wider than baseline — covering player folds ~80%, you pressure the rest

## Decision Rules
- **BTN 9-12bb, covered by heaps**: ~20% VPIP. Fold ~80%. No open shoves. Offsuit Ax very poor.
- **BTN 9-12bb, close stacks**: ~28% VPIP. More open shoving. BB handicapped by risk.
- **BTN 33-40bb, covered**: ~35% VPIP. Don't over-tighten. BB must risk a lot to pressure you.
- **UTG 16-29bb, covered by heaps**: ~7-9% VPID. Fold nines, AJo, KQo, A9s in some configs.
- **Covering the BB helps even if covered behind**: Still wider than baseline — covering player folds ~80%.
- **Very short / micro stacks at other tables**: Tighten further (FGS overlay — folding has positive $EV).
- **You're the shortest at the table**: Loosen back up — can't rely on others busting.

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| BB covers you by heaps | Open very tight — they can destroy you postflop |
| Very short / micro stacks at other tables | Tighten (FGS — folding has positive $EV) |
| Covered by massive stack (120bb+) when mid | Trim thinnest hands; no wider than ~35% on BTN |
| BB is close to your stack (game of chicken) | Open a bit wider — BB handicapped by risk |
| You're deeper (30-50bb) and covered | More forgiving — losing raise-fold costs <10% of stack |
| You're the shortest at the table | Loosen — can't rely on others busting |

## Sizing
- Min-raise dominant when covered (preserve tournament life, fold to reshoves)
- Open shoves drop sharply — and shift stronger (AQ, not AJ) when they do appear
- BB 3-bet size vs covering opener can be large/polar (~14bb) to deny price with Ax

## Heuristics
- "Same stack, different range — BB's depth drives your open, not just yours"
- "Covering the BB helps even if covered behind — pressure the rest"
- "Folding is not zero EV on the bubble — shorter stacks elsewhere = positive $EV for folding"
- "Min-raise when covered — preserve tournament life"
- "Open shoves shift stronger (AQ not AJ) when covered"

## Rules Q&A
- Q: What VPIP do you open at 12bb BTN into a 53bb BB (covered by heaps)?
  Options: [~20% — almost no open shoves, offsuit Ax very poor, ~35%, ~9%, ~50%]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Covered by heaps → ~20%. Cut low pairs, worst Ax. Common leak: autopilot shoving K7s/T9s/KTo.
- Q: What happens at 30-40bb covered?
  Options: [Don't over-tighten — ~35%. BB must risk a lot to pressure you, Play very tight ~9%, Open shove everything, Fold most hands]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Common leak: way too tight at 30-40bb covered. Losing a raise-fold costs <10% of stack. Still ~35%.
- Q: What is the "game of chicken" dynamic?
  Options: [Close stacks → BB handicapped by risk → you open wider (~28%), You fold everything, You jam every hand, You limp]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: BTN 12bb vs BB 16bb: BB risks tournament life playing back. You open wider with more open-shoving.
- Q: What is the common leak at 12bb covered?
  Options: [Autopilot shoving K7s/T9s/KTo (too wide for covered stacks), Over-tightening, Limping too much, Not shoving enough]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Covered short → offsuit Ax very poor, almost no open shoves. Autopilot shoving suited connectors/broadways is a leak.
- Q: What is the dominant sizing when covered?
  Options: [Min-raise (preserve tournament life, fold to reshoves), Open-shove, Limp, 3x raise]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Min-raise dominant when covered. Open shoves drop sharply and shift stronger (AQ, not AJ).
- Q: What happens to low pairs (22-33) when covered and short?
  Options: [Often fold — they block the blinds' pair-folds, hurting fold equity, Always open, Always shove, Limp]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Low pairs block the blinds' pair-folds, reducing your fold equity. When covered and short, often fold.
