# BM10 — UTG Covers BB (Postflop)

## Overview
UTG covers BB on the bubble. UTG c-bets very high (>90% across hundreds of drilled hands). BB defends very tight. Tighter EP ranges increase the risk of running into nuts, reducing BB's check-raise frequency. Strong value threshold for flop check-raises.

## Buckets
- **UTG c-bet (covering)**: Very high frequency (>90%). Range bet most boards. Few checks. Drop a few points when deeper.
- **BB defense (covered)**: Very tight preflop → strong postflop range. But tighter EP range = more risk of running into nuts = reduced check-raise.
- **Check-shove low boards**: BB can check-shove (like 99 on 9-high) in LP spots, but this DISAPPEARS as opener gets tighter (EP).

## Decision Rules
- **UTG c-bets >90%**: Very likely above 90% across most boards. Few checks. Anecdotally from hundreds of drilled hands.
- **Drop a few points when deeper**: At 100bb, some low boards check back (BB has better coverage). At short/covering by 2x, range bet almost everything.
- **Low board coverage**: BB has better coverage on low boards when deeper. UTG must be more careful — can't range back with impunity on every board.
- **BB check-raise threshold is strong**: Be very careful with flop check-raises for value. KT on K84 = pure check-call in short stack scenario. Not like chips — can't play to maximize chips.
- **Tighter EP range = less check-shoving**: 99 on 9-high = check-shove in LP spots. Disappears vs EP (UTG has stronger range, more risk of running into nuts).
- **Non-all-in river sizings**: Important on the bubble. Play with AI for sizings. Make "asking why" a habit.
- **BB's tighter range can have MORE flushes on some turns**: After checking flop, donks on monotone turns. BB's tight preflop range means more suited hands → more flushes on 3-flush turns.
- **Strong value threshold**: Don't get carried away with check-raises. Balance protection with tournament life.

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| EP opener (tighter range) | BB check-raise frequency drops — more risk of running into nuts |
| Deeper stacks (100bb) | Some low boards check back — BB has better coverage |
| Short/covering by 2x | Range bet almost everything — BB must play defensively |
| Close stacks | BB can put pressure on UTG — both players more careful |
| Monotone turns (after checking flop) | BB may donk — tighter range has more flushes |

## Sizing
- UTG: range bet small sizing (standard for high-frequency c-bet)
- BB: non-all-in river sizings (leave tournament equity behind)
- Split sizings possible when deeper on specific textures

## Common Leaks
- BB check-raising too wide for value (KT on K84 is pure check-call)
- Not recognizing that tighter EP ranges reduce check-shove frequency on low boards
- Playing to maximize chips instead of balancing protection with tournament life
- Not implementing non-all-in river sizings on the bubble
