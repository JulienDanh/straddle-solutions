# BM5 — Blinds Facing Open (Bubble)

## Overview
Defense from SB and BB versus EP (UTG) and LP (BTN) opens on the bubble. Short blind stacks play almost pure raise/fold (no cold calls). Deeper stacks and covering stacks introduce cold calls. BB re-steals aggressively when covered by a wide opener.

## Buckets
- **SB short (≤20bb)**: ~0% cold calls. Raise/fold only. 3-bet value: Kings+, AQ (Queens mix).
- **SB deeper (25+bb)**: Some cold calls begin, especially if BB is short/handcuffed.
- **BB short (≤15bb)**: Very tight defense. Some cold calls. Fold dominated offsuit.
- **BB covering opener**: Defend wide. Cold call looser (fold dominated offsuit). Re-steal aggressively with Ax blockers.

## Decision Rules
- **SB cold-call threshold**: ~20bb. Below: ~0% cold calls, raise/fold only.
- **SB cold calls wider when BB is short**: BB is handcuffed (can't squeeze/lead). SB "almost in the BB" — realizes more equity.
- **Re-steal > cold call when covered**: Win outright more often AND avoid losing postflop ~50%+ of the time.
- **3-bet sizing UP in ICM**: Larger 3-bets deny equity to speculative calls and lower SPR for narrow value.
- **BB 3-bet value threshold (covered vs EP)**: Kings+, AK (Queens mix). Tighter than ChipEV.
- **BB defends wider than ChipEV vs tight UTG**: UTG opens ~8% on bubble. BB can donk-lead low/mid boards that miss UTG's range.
- **Re-steal MORE when covered and short**: It feels terrible to shove A5s and bust. But playing too passive reduces dollar EV — you cash slightly more often but never double. Think dollar EV, not binary cash/fail.

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| BB covers both opener and SB | SB cold calls shrink — BB can squeeze/lead post |
| BB is short | SB cold calls more — BB can't apply pressure (handcuffed) |
| BTN opens 70%+ (BB short) | SB cold-call range widens — SB "almost in the BB" |
| Squeeze when SB can't cold call | BB squeezes very liberally — cold calls don't exist |

## Sizing
- 3-bet sizing increases in high ICM from the blinds. Size up — deny equity, lower SPR.
- BTN open ~71% when BB is short (vs ~53% equal 50bb).

## Common Leaks
- Playing too passive when covered and short — "feels terrible to bust" but reduces dollar EV
- Not re-stealing enough with Ax blockers when covered
- Cold calling too wide from the blinds at short stacks
- Not recognizing when BB is handcuffed (can't squeeze) — SB should cold call more

## Heuristics
- "Re-steal MORE, not less, when covered and short — think dollar EV"
- "SB cold-call threshold: ~20bb"
- "3-bet sizing UP in ICM — deny equity, lower SPR"
- "BB covering opener = defend wide, re-steal aggressively"
- "Re-steal > cold call when covered — win outright AND avoid postflop"
