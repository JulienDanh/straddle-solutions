# System 6 — Check-Raising Top Pair

## Overview
Hero flops top pair from the BB at short stack depth. Core insight: below 35bb, the check-raising range changes dramatically — top pair hands become check-raises, not check-calls. Two pair and better become traps (check-call).

## Buckets
- **Top pair (value protection)**: Check-raise — needs protection, gets stacks in over 2 streets
- **Two pair / sets (traps)**: Check-call — SPR is so low you can shove the river without raising
- **Draws (bluffs)**: Check-raise with gut shots, flush draws, backdoor draws with a heart

## Decision Rules
- **35bb inflection point**: Below 35bb, heavily consider check-raising top pair. Above 35bb, check-raising becomes nuts-oriented (sets, two pair).
- **Check-raise top pair**: KQ, KJ, KT down to K2 — the weaker the kicker, the more it needs protection
- **Check-call (trap) two pair and better**: At short stacks, SPR is narrow enough to shove river without raising
- **Backdoor flush draw = check-call**: Top pair with two cards of a suit (backdoor flush draw) can check-call instead of check-raise (doesn't need protection as much)
- **Lower flops = less kicker matters**: On a 6-high flop where villain can't have suited 6s or 5s, your kicker is irrelevant — check-raise all top pairs
- **Higher flops = hierarchical**: On a Q-high flop, check-raise tapers from KQ → QJ → QT → Q9 → Q8 → Q2

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Backdoor flush draw (two cards of same suit) | Check-call — doesn't need protection as much |
| Higher flop | Kicker matters more — hierarchical taper from top kicker down |
| Lower flop | Kicker matters less — villain can't make their own top pair |
| Three-to-straight on board | 3-to-straight top pairs may check-raise more (replaces slightly stronger top pairs) |
| Donk betting present | If BB donk bets, the c-bettor's range is stronger — need stronger hands to CR |
| Villain c-bets too wide | Check-raise ALL top pairs (they're range is too weak) |

## Sizing
- Small check-raise (to ~6bb into 6bb pot) — keeps villain's range wide
- After CR, shove or bet small on innocuous turns

## Blocker Effects
- Ace of hearts is a heavily utilized bluff (direct out to king + flush draw blocker reduces jam frequency)
- High card heart > low card heart for bluffing (usually)
- On K94 with clubs: K2♣ (flush draw) = trap (check-call). K7 no club = check-raise (value protection).

## Common Leaks
- Check-calling top pair at short stacks — should be check-raising for value protection
- Overvaluing position — people think "EP opened so I need TPTK" — stack depth matters more than position
- Not recognizing the 35bb inflection point — the game changes dramatically below it
- Trapping with top pair instead of two pair — top pair needs protection, two pair can trap

## Hand Examples
- **K7 on Q75 (HJ, 25bb)**: Pure check-raise. KQ, QJ, QT all pure CR. Q9 heavy mix, Q8 medium, Q7 small mix, Q2 pure call. Hierarchical taper.
- **Q9 on Q75 (BTN, 20bb)**: Check-raise. QJ pure CR, QT pure CR, Q9 heavy CR, Q8 mix. Backdoor flush draw (Q9♥) = check-call.
- **K7 on K84 (EP, 13bb)**: Pure check-raise. KQ, KJ, KT, K9 all pure CR. K7, K6, K5, K3, K2 all pure CR. K8 (two pair) = trap (check-call).
- **K3♣ on K94 (HJ, 15bb)**: Check-call (trap). K9♣, K4♣, 94♣ = all traps. K7 no club = check-raise (protection). Flush draw hands trap, non-flush-draw top pairs CR.
- **65o on 864r (CO, 25bb)**: Pure check-raise (top pair + gut shot). Need protection. But CO's range is strong on this low board — must be more careful. 85, 87 with gut shots = good CR. T8, J8 = not as good (weaker draws).
- **QJ on J96 (BTN, 25bb)**: Pure check-raise. Half-pot bet from villain doesn't change this. J9, J2 (two pair) = traps (check-call). J3 (no kicker) = check-call. J4 = mostly check-call.

## Heuristics
- "35bb = inflection point — below it, check-raise top pair"
- "Top pair check-raises, two pair traps"
- "Shorter = more check-raising"
- "Lower flop = kicker matters less"
- "Backdoor flush draw = check-call (doesn't need protection)"
- "Overestimate position, underestimate stack depth = big mistake"

## Rules Q&A
- Q: What is the inflection point for check-raising top pair?
  Options: [35 big blinds, 20 big blinds, 50 big blinds, 10 big blinds]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Below 35bb, the SPR is narrow enough to stack off with one pair. Top pair check-raises for protection. Above 35bb, CR ranges become nuts-oriented.
- Q: At short stacks, what should you do with two pair?
  Options: [Check-call (trap) — SPR is low enough to shove river without raising, Check-raise always, Fold, Donk bet]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: At 13bb, after check-calling, you can shove the river. No need to raise — trapping is more profitable. Top pair needs protection (CR), two pair doesn't (trap).
- Q: What effect does a backdoor flush draw have on top pair?
  Options: [Shifts toward check-call — doesn't need protection as much, Shifts toward check-raise, No effect, Makes it a fold]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Top pair with two cards of a suit (e.g. Q9♥ on Q75) can check-call. The backdoor flush draw means the hand doesn't need as much protection — it has backup equity.
- Q: Why does kicker matter less on lower flops?
  Options: [Villain can't make their own top pair (suited 6s aren't in their range at 25bb), Villain always has the nuts, Kickers never matter, Low flops always favor villain]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: At 25bb, HJ's bottom suited hand is suited 7s. On a 6-high flop, HJ can't have top pair. So your kicker doesn't matter — they can't outkick you.
- Q: When villain c-bets too wide on a low board, what happens to your top pair check-raises?
  Options: [Check-raise ALL top pairs — villain's range is too weak, Check-call only, Fold top pair, Only check-raise the nuts]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: If villain c-bets 100% on a board they mostly miss (like 864 vs CO), their range is weak. All top pairs become check-raises.
- Q: What is the check-raise sizing for short stacks?
  Options: [Small (~3x the c-bet), Pot-sized, Min-raise, All-in]
  Correct: 0
  Explanation: Small check-raise keeps villain's range wide. After CR, shove or bet small on innocuous turns. Short stacks = 2-street game.
