# BM6 — Dealing With 3-Bets (Bubble)

## Overview
Facing a 3-bet on the bubble. The core skill: tighten your call range dramatically vs ChipEV. ICM amplifies the cost of calling and losing. Most hands that are calls in ChipEV become folds on the bubble.

## Buckets
- **Fold**: Most of the range. Marginal hands that call in ChipEV fold on the bubble.
- **Call**: Only premium hands — top of your opening range. Narrow.
- **4-bet/Jam**: Very narrow value range. Highly polarized.

## Decision Rules
- **Tighten call range dramatically**: Hands like AQ, JJ, TT that call in ChipEV become folds on the bubble. The 3-bettor has a strong, polar range and ICM pressure amplifies the cost of calling.
- **4-bet/Jam range narrows**: Only the top of your range — AA, KK, QQ (mix), AK. Much tighter than ChipEV.
- **Position matters**: Calling is slightly more viable in position (realize equity better) but still very tight.
- **Stack depth matters**: At 15bb, mostly jam/fold. At 40bb+, some calls become viable with premium hands.
- **Who 3-bets matters**: A 3-bet from a covering stack is more dangerous than from a covered stack (they risk more).

## Risk Factors
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Covered by the 3-bettor | More cautious — they risk more, represent stronger range |
| Shorter stacks | Mostly jam/fold — no room to call and fold |
| ICM pressure (direct bubble) | Maximum tightness — cost of losing is highest |
| Position | Slightly more viable IP (realize equity), still very tight |

## Sizing
- 4-bet jamming is common at short stacks
- At deeper stacks, 4-bet non-all-in sizing may appear but value range stays narrow

## Heuristics
- "Tighten dramatically vs ChipEV — most calls become folds"
- "4-bet/Jam range: AA, KK, QQ (mix), AK — very narrow"
- "ICM amplifies the cost of calling and losing"
- "Covering stack 3-bets are more dangerous — they risk more"
